## 2. New: degradation with non-realizability — E3
Goal: measure how fast the CN algorithms degrade as an LCN moves away from family-realizability.
- `cve` is exact for D_SE, so its gap to certified `cjt_scip` is the **relaxation error alone**.
- `ivbp`, `cdve` and `cve_eps` add their own algorithmic error on top.

### 2.1 Generator extension: controlled perturbation (`Generator.perturb`)
Start from a shipped `-fr` instance, which fixes the topology, sentences and witness. Then add `m`
non-realizable sentences of a chosen kind:
- **NR1 non-root marginal:** Type-1 `P(x)` on a non-root atom.
- **NR2 mixture conditional:** Type-2 `P(x | ψ)` with ψ over a strict subset of the parents, or a
  disjunction, so it is satisfied by ≥ 2 parent configurations.
- **NR3 cross-family:** a Type-1/Type-2 sentence whose scope spans two families (e.g. siblings, or a
  grandparent and a grandchild). It is out of every family's scope, so the compiled CN ignores it.

Bounds are `[v − δ, v + δ]` around its value `v` under the base instance's product witness, which
satisfies every sentence and every LMC assertion. So consistency is preserved by construction, and
the half-width δ controls how binding the sentence is.

Each perturbed instance must still be CJT-exact (`cjt_exactness_report`, structural). NR3 can widen
the tree; if the gate fails, resample.

`realizability_report` reports the realized fraction of non-realizable sentences per instance.

Files: `benchmarks/nr_{topology}_n{n}/{base}_nr{kind}_m{m}_d{δ}.lcn`, with the parameters also
written as `#` header comments. A new `--group-by nr` dimension in `analyze_results.py` reads them.
Script: `lcn/benchmarks/gen_nonrealizable.py`.

### 2.2 Design
- **Main sweep:**
  - `tree` and `dag` (`dag` is loopy, so IVBP is a heuristic there);
  - n ∈ {10, 20}, the 10 base instances per size;
  - m ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4, 8} (m = 0 is the base);
  - a mixed kind (NR1/NR2/NR3 round-robin), δ = 0.1.
  - This gives 2 × 2 × 10 × 4 = 160 new instances.
- **Kind × tightness ablation:** `dag`, n = 10, m = 4, each kind separately, δ ∈ {0.05, 0.3} →
  10 × 3 × 2 = 60 instances.
- **Runs:** `cjt_scip` (reference), `cve`, `cve_eps_0.1`, `ivbp`, `cdve`, `ariel`.
- **Evidence:** K = 0, the same as the main grid.
- **Budget:** about 220 instances; at n ≤ 20, `cjt_scip` takes about 10–20 s per instance.
  Roughly 3 serial hours.

### 2.3 Metrics and presentation
Plotted against m (and against the realized non-realizable fraction):
- MAE of the lower and upper bounds;
- width ratio (method width / reference width);
- outer and inner rates (tol 1e-4).

The expected picture:
- CVE stays **outer** (DAG-structured ⇒ factorizing ⇒ `prop:inclusion`) and widens with m.
- IVBP matches it on trees and is a heuristic on dags.
- CDVE is inner for D_SE, with no guarantee vs D_LCN.
- ARIEL operates on the LCN itself.

Presentation:
- **Main text:** one compact figure (MAE / width ratio vs m, tree and dag, K = 0) plus 2–3
  sentences.
- **Appendix:** the full sweep and the kind × δ table.

The realistic sets (real, junkyu) are the "naturally" non-realizable end of the same axis: report
their non-realizable fraction next to Table 1.
